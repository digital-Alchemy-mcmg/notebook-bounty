# Candidate Alpha — Project Notes

All people, organizations, and events in this file are fictional.

## Northwind migration

Mira Chen joined the Northwind migration as delivery lead on 8 January 2024.
The team moved 14 internal services during the first release window. The release
window closed on 2 February 2024 with two services deferred after dependency
reviews.

## Training pilot

In March 2024, Mira designed a three-session onboarding pilot for 18 analysts.
Attendance records show that 16 analysts completed all three sessions. A survey
was offered after the pilot, but the notes do not report a response rate.

## Budget note

The approved pilot budget was USD 12,000. A draft status note listed projected
spend of USD 10,800; it did not identify final expenditure.
